# PrepAI — Student Placement Preparation User Guide

---

## 1. Welcome to PrepAI
**PrepAI** is your personal AI technical interviewer designed to prepare you for campus placement interviews. It conducts a realistic, conversational 5-stage job interview that adapts to your answers, tests your problem-solving depth, and gives you actionable feedback on how to improve.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Interview Preparation Guide

### Step 1: Create Your Account & Log In
1. Open the PrepAI portal in **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge**.
2. Click **"Get Started"** or **"Sign Up"**.
3. Register using your email address and choose a secure password.
4. Your account is activated immediately — no waiting for verification emails!
5. Log in and head straight to your dashboard.

---

### Step 2: Set Up Your Profile & Resume
1. Navigate to **"Profile"** from the top navigation bar.
2. Upload your latest PDF or DOCX resume, or paste your resume text directly.
3. *Tip*: PrepAI automatically extracts your claimed skills (e.g. Python, React, PostgreSQL, Docker) to test you on technologies listed on your resume!

---

### Step 3: Choose Your Target Interview
1. Click **"Start Interview"** from the dashboard.
2. Select your **Target Job Role** (e.g. *Backend Developer*, *Frontend Developer*, *Full Stack Engineer*, *ML/AI Engineer*).
3. Select your **Experience Level** (e.g. *0-2 years* for fresh graduates).
4. Select your **Interview Focus** (*Technical*, *System Design*, or *Behavioral*).
5. Click **"Proceed to System Check"**.

---

### Step 4: Hardware Diagnostic & System Check
1. In the System Check room, allow browser permissions for your **Camera** and **Microphone**.
2. You will see a 4-point diagnostic:
   - **Optical Framing (Camera)**: Checks that your face is centered and clearly visible.
   - **Audio Input (Microphone)**: Confirms your microphone is capturing voice waveform.
   - **Speech Recognition**: Prepares live voice-to-text.
   - **Secure Session**: Verifies your connection to the database.
3. *Note*: If your camera or microphone is unavailable on your device, **don't worry!** You can still enter the interview and use typed responses. Missing hardware will **never** reduce your technical score.
4. Click **"Enter Interview Studio"**.

---

### Step 5: The 5-Stage Live Interview
Your interview will progress through 5 structured stages:

1. **Stage 1 (Fundamentals)**: Tests your understanding of core concepts and language/database mechanics.
2. **Stage 2 (Applied Scenarios)**: Probes real-world implementation, framework design, and technologies from your resume.
3. **Stage 3 (Deep Technical & Scale)**: Explores concurrency, race conditions, and performance trade-offs.
4. **Stage 4 (System Design)**: Tests your architectural thinking for scalable, fault-tolerant distributed systems.
5. **Stage 5 (Behavioral Leadership)**: Evaluates personal ownership, team collaboration, and crisis response using the **STAR Method** (Situation, Task, Action, Result).

---

### Step 6: Answering Questions & Live Telemetry
- **Speak Naturally**: Click the **Microphone icon** and speak clearly into your mic. Your words will appear live in the transcript box.
- **Pace & Fillers**: The live pill displays your active Speaking Pace (WPM) and count of filler words (e.g. *um*, *like*, *you know*).
- **Substance Over Length**: A concise, direct answer that explains *how and why* something works will score higher than a long, vague answer filled with buzzwords.
- Click **"Submit Response"** to submit your answer and view immediate qualitative feedback.
- Click **"Next Question"** to proceed.

---

### Step 7: Reviewing Your Performance Audit Report
Upon completing Stage 5, click **"Finish & View Audit"** to open your comprehensive report:

- **Overall Score**: Weighted as **85% Content Performance** and **15% Communication Delivery**.
- **Hiring Readiness Classification**: Tells you whether you are *Needs Significant Preparation*, *Developing*, or *Interview Ready for Target Role*.
- **Dimension "Why" Rationales**: Clear, evidence-backed explanations for your Technical Accuracy, Explanation Depth (Level 1–5), Reasoning, and Clarity.
- **Validated Resume Skills**: Highlights skills you proved working knowledge of vs topics requiring further revision.
- **Actionable Delivery Coaching**: Non-judgmental tips to improve your speaking pace and camera presence.
- **Download PDF**: Click **"Download Official Audit PDF"** to save or share your interview results.

## 3. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Are my webcam video or microphone audio recorded and stored?**  
**A:** No. All computer vision and audio analytics are processed 100% locally inside your browser. No video frames or audio recordings are uploaded or saved to any server.

**Q: What if my webcam is broken or I don't have microphone permissions?**  
**A:** You can type all your answers in the transcript box. The system automatically marks camera/voice as offline and evaluates your technical score purely on your answer content.

**Q: How does the AI evaluate behavioral questions?**  
**A:** PrepAI looks for the **STAR framework**: clearly state the **Situation** (context), the **Task** (your goal), the **Action** (what you personally did), and the **Result** (the measurable outcome).
